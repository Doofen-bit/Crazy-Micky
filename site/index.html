Read the evidence carefully

These are exploratory reanalysis results. DESeq2 fits three phenotypes separately within each region and sampling time. Positive log₂ fold change means higher expression in the first named group. Changing cutoffs reclassifies stored results; it does not refit models or rerun GO enrichment.

Missing is not zero. Genes without adjusted p-values are omitted from the volcano's y-axis and remain searchable. Independent filtering was computed at α = .05. Low-count filtering can change the tested universe across contexts, so DEG counts also depend on power and gene availability.

Scope. Male mice, small group sizes, bulk tissue, and a single experimental dataset limit generalization. Significance in one time point but not another does not establish a time-by-phenotype interaction. Cross-region samples from one animal are paired; no cross-region inference is made here.
